Academics

Academic Programmes

CFMA offers a full secondary education curriculum aligned with the Nigerian National Policy on Education, covering JSS and SSS levels.

Junior Secondary (JSS)

JSS 1 – JSS 3 covering foundation subjects. Students sit the Basic Education Certificate Examination (BECE).

Senior Secondary (SSS)

SS 1 – SS 3 with Science, Commercial and Art streams. Students prepare for WAEC SSCE and NECO.

📚 JSS Subjects Offered

English Language Mathematics Igbo Language Basic Science Basic Technology Social Studies Civic Education Christian Religious Studies Business Studies Home Economics Agricultural Science Physical & Health Education Computer Studies (ICT) Cultural & Creative Arts French Language Security Education Nigerian History Vocational & Technical Studies

🎓 SSS Subjects Offered

English Language ★ Mathematics ★ Igbo Language Physics Chemistry Biology Further Mathematics Financial Accounting Commerce Economics Marketing Government Literature in English Christian Religious Studies Agricultural Science Computer Studies (ICT) Office Practice
